{% load i18n %} {% comment %} One borrower, in tabs. The page this replaced said four things stacked down a long scroll: who they are, what they are holding, what they are waiting for, and what they have had. They are four separate questions, and a librarian has one of them at a time - so they are four tabs, and the answer is on screen without scrolling past the other three. There are no inline links in the tables. Inside a dialog a link is a trap: following it throws away the dialog and whatever the reader was in the middle of. So the tables are plain text, and the things worth going to have proper buttons in the footer, one each. Dual out-of-band titles, the same as the loan dialog: this one fragment is opened both by a row click (into #globalModal) and by a link on another page (into #formModal), and only the one that exists gets swapped.
