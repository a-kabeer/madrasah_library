{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The physical side of a new book: volumes, how many copies of each, and where they go. This is the part that saves the librarian walking Book -> Volume -> Copy -> Shelf by hand. It is deliberately plain markup: with JavaScript running, app.js shows one step at a time, keeps the copy rows in step with the volume rows, and reloads the Shelf list when the Location changes. Without it every section is simply visible at once and a single-volume book with a copy count still saves correctly — one long form rather than a broken one.
